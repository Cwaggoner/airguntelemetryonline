Key Variables

  • Muzzle velocity — measured with a chronograph; primary driver of trajectory.
  • Ballistic coefficient (BC) — how efficiently a projectile retains velocity.
  • Zero distance — the range at which the line of sight and trajectory intersect.
  • Environmental factors — altitude, temperature, humidity, and wind affect drop and drift.

Energy

Kinetic energy is commonly calculated as:

Energy (ft-lbs) = (velocity² × weight in grains) / 450240

For airguns this helps compare power between different pellets and tunes.

Practical Advice

Chronograph your actual loads. Published BC values and factory velocities are starting points only. Build a dope card from your own measured data and confirmed hits.
