Airgun Online Telemetry

Telemetry app for Air Gun enthusiasts

About

A place for airgun shooters to log, track, and analyze performance data — muzzle velocity, energy, shot strings, pellet/slug choices, and consistency across different setups.

Planned Features

Shot String Logging Record chronograph data with timestamps and notes.
Energy & Velocity Automatic ft-lbs / joules calculations from velocity and pellet weight.
Gun & Pellet Profiles Save rifles, tunes, and preferred ammunition.
Graphs & Trends Visualize consistency, velocity curves, and session comparisons.
