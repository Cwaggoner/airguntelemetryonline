Holdover vs Dialing

A holdover is an angular correction applied by aiming off the center of the reticle (using a known hash mark or estimated amount). Dialing means turning the elevation or windage turret to move the reticle so the center point is correct for the distance.

Basic Process

  1. Know your zero distance and the drop/wind value for the target distance (from a ballistic app, dope card, or experience).
  2. Convert that linear correction (inches or cm) into angular units (MOA or mils) for the distance.
  3. Either dial the required amount or hold the corresponding reticle mark.

Example (MOA, approximate)

At 300 yards a round drops 12 inches relative to a 100-yard zero. 12 inches at 300 yards is roughly 4 MOA (12 / 3). With 1/4 MOA clicks that is 16 clicks up, or a 4 MOA holdover.

These are simplified examples. Real dope depends on velocity, BC, altitude, temperature, and other factors. Use verified data for your system.
