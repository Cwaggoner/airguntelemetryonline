Overview

Both milliradians (mils) and Minutes of Angle (MOA) are angular units used for scope adjustments and ranging. Neither is inherently more accurate; the difference is in the unit size and how the reticle or turrets are graduated.

Key Differences

UnitApprox. at 100 ydCommon Click Value
1 MOA~1.047 inches1/4 MOA
0.1 mil~0.36 inches0.1 mil

1 mil is approximately 3.6 inches at 100 yards (or 3.6 MOA). Many mil scopes use 0.1 mil clicks.

Practical Notes

  • MOA is often preferred by hunters and some precision shooters for its familiar inch-based mental math at common distances.
  • Mils are common in military and competition precision rifle contexts because the reticle and turret units match more cleanly for ranging formulas.
  • Choose the system that matches your optic and training; mixing systems requires conversion.

General reference only. Confirm your optic’s exact subtension and click values.
